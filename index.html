Agentic AI command centers for manufacturing

Three clickable product prototypes I designed during my Tech Mahindra internship (2026) to show enterprise clients how multi-agent AI would run their operations, iterated with business-unit heads until ready for client demos.

Harshwardhan Patil, MBA, DoMS IIT Roorkee

These are front-end prototypes. Data, model predictions and agent actions are simulated to demonstrate the product experience and decision workflow.

  • ScaleAI Command

    For a Head of AI CoE at a marine-engine OEM

    An AI portfolio operating system that moves initiatives from pilot to P&L: tracks 14 AI initiatives, flags EU AI Act risk, models inference spend, and surfaces what to scale next.

    Designed AI: portfolio-monitoring agent, drift detection, compliance drafting. Built with HTML, JavaScript, Leaflet.

    Open demo

  • PredictProcure Command

    For a plant head at an automotive plant

    A plant nerve center linking machine-failure prediction to spare-parts procurement, with explainable-AI reasoning, a what-if simulator and a multi-agent orchestration view.

    Designed AI: predictive-maintenance ensemble, sourcing and logistics agents. Built with HTML, JavaScript, Chart.js.

    Open demo

  • InBound Command

    For an inbound-logistics head across three plants

    A supply-chain control tower that tracks shipments, ranks alerts by cost of risk, auto-heals routine exceptions and hands decisions to an AI co-pilot crew.

    Designed AI: alerting and co-pilot agent crew, weather-aware route risk. Built with HTML, JavaScript, Chart.js, Leaflet.
